Help

My profile says “awaiting verification”

Home Affairs checks your national ID against the civil register before you can apply for anything. It usually takes one working day. You will get a notification the moment it clears.

Do I have to upload the same certificate again?

No. Once an officer marks a document verified, it sits in your document wallet and every connected department can read it. That is the point of the system.

Who can see my records?

Each department is cleared for specific parts of your profile and nothing more. Traffic sees your licence and vehicles; it does not see your tax account. Finance sees payments; it does not see your police clearance.

Every time an officer opens your record, the system writes their name, department and the time to the audit log.

I paid but my application has not moved

Payments post immediately and push the case to “Under review”. If yours has not, open the application and check the payment status, then quote the receipt reference to the department.

I have no smartphone

The system runs in any browser, including on the shared computers at district offices. An officer at the counter can also submit on your behalf and print your receipt.
