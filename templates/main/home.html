How an application moves

You apply

Pick a service. Your details fill in from your verified profile, and documents already on file are reused.

You pay

Settle the fee by mobile money or bank transfer and get a receipt with a reference number.

The department works it

An officer claims the case, checks what their department is cleared to see, and records a decision.

You collect

Status changes reach you as a notification, so you travel to the office once — to collect.
